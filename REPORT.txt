BRYNZ TECHNICAL REPORT - PROTOTYPE, NOT ASSESSMENT READY

1. ARCHITECTURE AND TIER DESIGN
Python 3.12, uv.lock, CPU only. Stray depth/poses/intrinsics feed a point-cloud
backend. RGB uses pycolmap CPU SIFT, exhaustive matching and bundle adjustment,
then Depth Anything V2 Metric Indoor Small, approximately 24.8M parameters / 99 MB.
Image preparation is capped at 960 pixels, video at 64 frames, photos at 8 per room.
Sparse triangulations anchor the learned metric-depth scale by median depth ratio.
Metric scale is learned, not physically observable from arbitrary unposed photos.
Mean image-camera-up provides an upright-image gravity prior, not measured gravity.
All room folders enter one shared solve. Disconnected reconstruction components are
not stitched by arbitrary placement; omitted/failed rooms retain null measurements.

2. GEOMETRY, DRIFT AND OUTPUT
The Stray export uses OpenCV camera coordinates. Intrinsics come from each frame;
depth is millimetres. Confidence <2 and ranges outside 0.2-5 m are discarded.
Depth uses every tenth frame and every third pixel. 2.5 cm voxels bound geometry
work; deterministic RANSAC extracts planes. Supported wall-axis peaks establish a
candidate Manhattan rectangle. The model cannot represent every irregular room.
Floor/ceiling must both have horizontal plane support to report ceiling height.
An observed-floor watershed can propose multiple non-overlapping room regions when
enclosing wall evidence exists; raster boundaries are not precision fitted walls.
Adjacency is a candidate shared-free-space or common-coordinate opening relation.
Failure to establish overlap-free adjacency fails the whole-property product gate.

Drift correction uses overlapping fragments (15 sampled frames), voxel nearest
neighbours, robust median translations bounded to 15 cm, and iterative alignment.
It is local translation-only ICP, not a full pose graph or global loop closure.
It can reduce overlap error but cannot establish true accumulated drift accuracy.
On/off ablation produces both plans, dimensions and correction metadata.
Cloud fragments and camera origins receive the same translation correction for
opening ray tests and depth-projected damage, keeping projection coordinates aligned.

Openings need rays crossing a candidate wall into geometry beyond it, with low
occupancy at the crossing. Gaps alone are not detections. Grid extent is a candidate
width; 8 cm minimum uncertainty does not meet the 2 cm assessment gate.
Missing floor/ceiling, openings or rooms are not fabricated from priors.
The output schema is LOCAL and provisional: published Round 1 schema unavailable.
PNG/SVG plans and NPZ clouds accompany schema-validated JSON.

3. DAMAGE, CONCEALED RULES AND SCOPE
LiDAR RGB screening uses HSV discoloration and elongated dark-line candidates on
ten keyframes. This is not semantic damage recognition and is not a trained model.
Depth projects candidate pixels near fitted walls; bounding-rectangle metric extent
and surface ID are emitted. Wood, furniture, patterns and shadows cause false flags.
Cross-frame deduplication is approximate. Depth/video frame-index sync is assumed.
RGB-only damage projection is currently unsupported. All damage is review_required.
Concealed-moisture flags retain the fired rule and never assert a hidden leak.
Scope items request inspection and carry area intervals; no final repair estimate
or automatic scope approval is produced. Staged-class benchmarks are missing.

4. ERROR BUDGET AND CALIBRATION
LiDAR dimensions carry >=4 cm / approximately 2% engineering bounds for fitted
rectangles, larger grid-boundary errors for watershed partitions. Height bound is
>=3 cm plus plane residuals. These are NOT calibrated statistical intervals.
Sources: device bias, intrinsics and lens distortion, tracking drift, surfaces not
observed, furniture, model assumptions and raster resolution. Distortion is logged
but not corrected. Gravity uncertainty is not fully propagated.
RGB bounds include >=12% for video / >=25% for photos plus scale median deviation;
this acknowledges weaker priors but does not establish empirical interval coverage.
Held-out physical GT is needed for calibration. The scorer reports interval coverage,
not a false calibrated-pass claim. LiDAR-relative pseudo-labels cannot prove accuracy.

5. BENCHMARK AND FIX LOOP
Available raw samples: single_room, nested single_scan_floor_only, and an incoming
single_scan_with_ceiling export. The latter ran separately in 39.81 seconds, with
candidate ceiling height 2.33231 m and uncalibrated bounds [2.23666, 2.42795] m.
It is supplemental evidence in out/ceiling, not physically measured accuracy.
RGB tests are derived from the first scan's video, not independent device captures.
Actual timings/results are in out/benchmark/benchmark.json; they are regenerated.
Both RGB tiers currently fail to establish an enclosing room footprint on samples.
Original LiDAR sample produces partial geometry; its ceiling is unobserved.
There are no physical accuracy scores for any sample.
No 3-room benchmark, staged damage, independent repeats, or incumbent app exports.
Head-to-head scoring helper exists but no app comparison has been executed.
Repeatability helper separates independent captures from one-scan subsampling.

A real loader defect was shipped: using raw ARKit sign flips on Stray's converted
OpenCV poses smears surfaces. The legacy mode regenerates the before run; corrected
mode regenerates the after run. The rectangular diagnostic recovered 0 versus 4 walls.
Both JSON/plans and an output diff are in out/fixloop; fix-source.diff shows the
source correction. Drift and watershed are disabled equally for both runs.
The declaration is explicitly
RETROSPECTIVE and not a pre-registered worst measured gate. No scored gate movement
or 25% fix-loop compliance is claimed. Watershed boundary counts are unstable
under small corrections and are not the isolated rectangular diagnostic metric.
The user requested no Git writes; checkpoint.json replaces only progress recording,
not the assessment's commit-history evidence. User must commit genuine milestones.

6. FAILURES, DEVICES, REPRODUCTION AND DISCLOSURES
Photos/video intended for iPhone 15+; LiDAR for LiDAR-equipped Pro devices.
Physical compatibility, stock protocol usability and install time are unverified.
Device matrix: device-matrix.json. Requirement status: compliance.json.
Photos with no overlapping views, pure rotation, repeated textures or thin room
coverage fail SfM. Learned metric depth cannot guarantee 8% photo or 3% video error.
Mirrors may produce apparent behind-wall hits; glass may have no depth. Wet glossy
surfaces and low light increase holes/outliers. Confidence filtering is insufficient
to distinguish all reflections. Furniture may be mistaken for wall/floor surfaces.
Alcoves and non-Manhattan rooms are not reliably reconstructed. Floor watershed can
oversegment furniture or merge a wide doorway. Disconnected photo rooms cannot have
their adjacency inferred uniquely; the pipeline reports insufficient evidence.

No remote inference. Model weights fetched by script/CLI, loaded locally, cached by
input/model hash. --live bypasses depth cache. Cache replay reproduces depth arrays;
bit-identical SfM result JSON across machines is NOT certified. LiDAR determinism is
tested locally. uv.lock pins installed packages; reproduction needs raw data too.
Model: depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-Indoor-Small-hf, fine-tuned on Hypersim.
Model source/card: https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-Indoor-Small-hf
